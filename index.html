Before You Book

Questions, answered straight.

How is Missed-Call Recovery different from the Workflow Audit?+

Missed-Call Recovery is a fixed-scope, 14-day build aimed at one specific leak — the phone. The Workflow Audit is for teams that want a broader look at everything before deciding what to fix first. Most people start with the free Leak Review either way; it tells us which one actually fits.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?+

No. Every engagement starts with a free Hours & Revenue Leak Review — a quick look at where your time and calls are going. You decide whether to move forward after that, one project at a time.

What if the automation breaks or stops working?+

Ongoing Support is optional but available — monitoring, updates, and quarterly check-ins so a fix doesn't quietly rot after launch. Even without a support plan, every build comes with documentation your team can hand to anyone.

How long does it actually take to go live?+

Missed-Call Recovery is built to go live in about 14 days after the review. A larger single-workflow build usually takes 2–4 weeks, depending on how many systems it touches — you get a straight timeline before any work starts.

What if the review finds nothing worth fixing?+

Then I'll tell you that. You'll have had an honest 30 minutes, not a sales pitch dressed up as a diagnosis. Hasn't happened yet — but that's the deal either way.

Do you only work with the trades listed above?+

HVAC, plumbing, auto repair, landscaping, and electrical are where most of the work is focused right now — not a hard boundary. If your business runs on repeatable calls, bookings, and paperwork, it's worth a conversation.

Is this just a ChatGPT wrapper?+

No. Some fixes use AI where it earns its keep — drafting replies, summarizing calls. A lot of them are plain automation — a text that sends on schedule, a form that files itself. You get whichever is more reliable, not whichever is trendier.

Do you work with clients outside the US?+

The current focus is US service businesses, but every engagement happens remotely over calls and screen-shares, so it can work outside the US too — ask on the Leak Review call. Pricing is quoted in USD regardless of location.
