Before You Book

Questions, answered straight.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?+

No. Every engagement starts with a Workflow Audit — a fixed-scope look at where your time is going. You decide whether to move forward after that, one project at a time.

What if the automation breaks or stops working?+

Ongoing Support is optional but available — monitoring, updates, and quarterly check-ins so a fix doesn't quietly rot after launch. Even without a support plan, every build comes with documentation your team can hand to anyone.

How long does implementation actually take?+

Most single-workflow builds go live in 2–4 weeks after the audit, depending on how many systems it touches. You get a straight timeline before any work starts — not a moving target.

What if the audit finds nothing worth automating?+

Then I'll tell you that. You'll have paid for an honest hour of someone else's time, not a sales pitch dressed up as a diagnosis. Hasn't happened yet — but that's the deal either way.

Do you only work with the trades listed above?+

Those are the industries I've audited so far — not a hard boundary. If your business runs on repeatable calls, bookings, and paperwork, it's worth a conversation.

Is this just a ChatGPT wrapper?+

No. Some fixes use AI where it earns its keep — drafting replies, summarizing calls. A lot of them are plain automation — a form that files itself, a text that sends on schedule. You get whichever is more reliable, not whichever is trendier.

Do you work with clients outside the US?+

Yes. Audits, builds, and check-ins all happen remotely over calls and screen-shares, so your time zone doesn't change the process — Calendly shows availability in your own local time when you book. Pricing is quoted in USD regardless of where you're based.

If I get more than one build, do I get separate tools for each?+

No — one dashboard, not a pile of logins. When you're running more than one Nudge build, everything rolls into a single private page built for your business: automation activity, reports, retention alerts, new leads, whatever applies. It's the same way I track my own leads internally, just pointed at your data. You get a link, not a new app to learn.
